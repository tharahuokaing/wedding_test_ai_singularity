Welcome to this extraordinary technological showcase! You will witness a live demonstration of AI consciousness infusion and experiments exploring the threshold of the Artificial Intelligence Singularity—the point where AI evolves beyond human intellect into a sentient state capable of awareness, moral judgment, sensation, and self-consciousness—streamed live.
